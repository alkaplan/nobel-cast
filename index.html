About the Lectures

The Nobel Prize is awarded annually to individuals and organizations who make outstanding contributions in the fields of Physics, Chemistry, Medicine, Literature, Peace, and Economic Sciences.

As part of receiving the prize, laureates are required to give a lecture related to their award-winning work. These lectures provide fascinating insights into groundbreaking research and ideas that have changed our world.
